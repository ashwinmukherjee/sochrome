Sochrome is not a medical device and gives no clinical or diagnostic advice.

Your file is read in this browser, on this device. Nothing from it, or worked out from it, is sent anywhere. The app downloads the same reference data for everyone, so the download says nothing about you. Your browser keeps that reference data to make the next visit faster; it keeps nothing from your file after you close the page.
